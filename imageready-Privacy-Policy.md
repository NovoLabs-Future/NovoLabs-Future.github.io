# ImageReady Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 3, 2026**

ImageReady (the “App”) is published by an individual developer (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

## 1. No account required

You do not need to create an ImageReady account or provide your name, phone number, or email address to use the App’s image tools.

## 2. Photos and image processing

ImageReady lets you select images for compression, batch compression, and format conversion. The app code is designed to process selected image data on your device. We do not operate a server to which ImageReady uploads your selected or processed images.

You choose which images to select and whether to save processed results to your Photos library. iOS controls the photo-selection and photo-saving permissions you grant to the App.

## 3. Local app data

The App stores limited operational information on your device, such as the daily free-use counter and its date, to enforce the daily allowance. This information is stored locally using Apple’s standard app storage facilities.

## 4. Purchases and subscriptions

ImageReady Pro purchases and subscription management are handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details or Apple Account password. Apple may process purchase and subscription information under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

## 5. Advertising and analytics

ImageReady V1 does not include advertising SDKs or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs. We do not use the App to serve targeted advertising or build behavioral advertising profiles.

## 6. Information handled by Apple and your device

Apple’s operating system, App Store, and purchase services may process technical, diagnostic, account, and transaction information as necessary to provide their services. That processing is governed by Apple’s terms and privacy policy, not by this policy alone.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

The local daily-use counter remains on your device while the App is installed. Deleting the App generally removes its local app data, subject to iOS behavior and any data you separately saved to Photos. Photos saved to your library remain under your control and must be managed in Photos.

## 8. Children

The App is a general-purpose image utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not require account registration.

## 9. Security

On-device image processing reduces the need to transmit personal images to a developer-operated server. No method of storage or processing can guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the App, its data practices, or applicable requirements. The updated policy will be published at the same policy URL with a revised effective date.
