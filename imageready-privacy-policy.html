ImageReady Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

ImageReady ("we", "our", or "the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how ImageReady handles information when you use our iOS application.

Our guiding principles are privacy, data minimization, and on-device image processing.

2. Information We Collect

ImageReady does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its core features.

ImageReady Version 1 does not include advertising SDKs or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs.

We do not intentionally collect personal information for advertising, profiling, or behavioral tracking.

3. Photos and Images

ImageReady provides image compression, batch compression, and image format conversion.

Image processing is designed to take place locally on your device. Your selected images are processed on your device, and we do not upload your original photos to developer-operated servers. Processed images are saved to your Photos library only when you choose to save them.

4. Permissions

ImageReady may request access to your photo library when you select images or save processed results. You can manage photo permissions at any time through iOS Settings. We use these permissions to provide the features you request.

5. Local Data Storage

ImageReady may store application settings and daily usage information locally on your device to support app functionality.

ImageReady does not require account registration or maintain a developer-operated user profile. Deleting the App may remove locally stored app data, subject to iOS behavior and data you independently saved to Photos.

6. Subscriptions and Payments

ImageReady Pro subscriptions are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. We do not collect or store your credit card details or Apple Account payment credentials.

Apple handles payment processing, subscription management, and applicable refund requests under its own policies.

For more information, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. Third-Party Services

ImageReady Version 1 does not include advertising SDKs or third-party behavioral analytics services. Apple's operating system and App Store services may process information necessary to provide app distribution, purchases, and subscription functionality under Apple's own privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We aim to minimize the information handled by ImageReady. On-device processing helps reduce the need to transmit personal images over the internet. No method of electronic storage or processing can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

ImageReady does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not require account registration or personal profile creation.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our features, technical implementation, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at our Privacy Policy URL.
