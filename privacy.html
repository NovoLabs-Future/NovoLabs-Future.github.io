Privacy Policy

PhotoClean – Smart Cleanup

Last updated: October 22, 2026

PhotoClean – Smart Cleanup ("PhotoClean") is operated by Qiang Guo, an individual developer, under the NovoFutureLabs brand.

We respect your privacy and design PhotoClean to help you manage your photo library without uploading your personal photos or videos to developer-operated servers for its core cleaning functions.

1. Photo Library Access

PhotoClean requests access to your photo library through Apple's permission system. This access is used to identify duplicate photos, screenshots, and large videos, display items for review, and perform deletions you authorize. You can manage PhotoClean's photo library permissions in your iPhone Settings.

2. On-Device Processing

PhotoClean's core scanning, analysis, and cleaning operations are designed to run on your iPhone. The app processes photo and video information locally to provide its cleaning features. Original photos and videos are not uploaded to servers operated by the developer for these functions.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

PhotoClean may store or temporarily process information needed for its functionality, including:

  • Photo and video counts and file sizes
  • Duplicate detection results and cleaning information
  • Scan history and cleaning progress
  • Cleaning preferences and free deletion usage

This information supports app operation and is stored or processed locally.

4. Developer Access

PhotoClean is designed so that the developer does not receive or remotely access your personal photo and video library through its core cleaning features.

5. Subscriptions and Apple Services

PhotoClean offers optional Pro subscriptions through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple handles payment processing, subscription transactions, and related account services under its own terms and privacy practices.

PhotoClean uses Apple's StoreKit framework to determine subscription eligibility and restore eligible purchases. PhotoClean does not require users to provide credit card details directly to the developer.

6. iCloud Photos

If you use iCloud Photos, Apple may synchronize or retrieve your media according to your device settings and Apple's services. This Apple-managed activity is separate from PhotoClean uploading media to developer-operated servers.

7. Advertising and Analytics

The current PhotoClean implementation is not intended to include third-party advertising trackers or third-party analytics services. Any future integration that changes data collection or sharing practices will require appropriate disclosure and updates to this policy.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

PhotoClean stores certain preferences and cleaning information locally to support its functionality. You may change photo access permissions through iPhone Settings.

Deleting the app generally removes its app-container data, subject to iOS behavior and any information stored separately by Apple or in device backups. Photos and videos deleted through PhotoClean are handled by Apple's Photos system and may remain in Recently Deleted according to Apple's retention rules.

9. Children's Privacy

PhotoClean is a general-purpose photo management utility and is not specifically directed at children. We do not intentionally design its core cleaning functions to collect children's personal information for transmission to developer-operated servers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to PhotoClean, applicable services, or legal requirements. The updated policy will include a revised date. Additional notice will be provided where required by applicable law.
