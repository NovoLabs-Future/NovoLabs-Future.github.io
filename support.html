Frequently Asked Questions

What does PhotoClean do?

PhotoClean helps you find duplicate photos, review screenshots, and identify large videos that may be taking up storage on your iPhone.

Does PhotoClean delete photos automatically?

No. You review and select items before deletion. iOS may ask you to confirm the deletion.

Where do deleted photos and videos go?

Deleted items generally move to the Recently Deleted album in Apple Photos, where they may remain for up to 30 days unless permanently deleted earlier.

Is PhotoClean free?

PhotoClean includes 10 free deletions shared across its three cleaning features. PhotoClean Pro offers unlimited cleaning through an auto-renewable subscription.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open PhotoClean, go to Settings, and select Restore Purchases. Make sure you are using the Apple account associated with your original purchase.

How can I manage or cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Select PhotoClean to manage your subscription.

Privacy & Security

PhotoClean analyzes your photo library on your iPhone. Your photos and videos are not uploaded to our servers. You remain in control of what you choose to delete.
